# Customer Help Assistant Guide

Open **Help Assistant** from supported storefront/dashboard pages. The mode
label is accurate:

- **Help Search** means deterministic local retrieval with no external AI;
- **AI Assistant** appears only if a future provider is truly active;
- unavailable means the backend gate is disabled or not reachable.

Anonymous users may ask about products, navigation, shipping/returns,
payment-method explanations, account help, and approved public information.
Signed-in customers may ask for their own recent order, payment, or refund
status. The assistant cannot access another customer, show private addresses or
complete payment references, submit checkout, alter an order, complete a
payment, approve a refund, or change a profile.

Answers show approved source labels. “Insufficient information” means no
approved source supports the question. Use the normal dashboard and configured
support channel. Always verify critical order, payment, and refund decisions in
the normal status screens.

Messages are not stored in localStorage/sessionStorage and server chat history
is not persisted. Do not submit passwords, tokens, payment credentials,
private addresses, or unnecessary personal information.
