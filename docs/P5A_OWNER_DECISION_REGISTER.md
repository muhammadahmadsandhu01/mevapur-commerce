## Approval completeness rule

P5 execution must not begin until every decision above has:

- an authorized owner-selected value;
- an approval identity and timestamp in the controlled approval system;
- any dependent platform facts attached;
- no conflict with the non-negotiable hard stops.

P5A leaves all owner selections and approvals **PENDING**.
