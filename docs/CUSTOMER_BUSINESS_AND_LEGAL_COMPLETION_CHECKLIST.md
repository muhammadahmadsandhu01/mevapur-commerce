# Customer Business and Legal Completion Checklist

Customer counsel and responsible business owners must complete and approve:

- legal/business display name, registration details, publishable address, and
  support channels;
- domain/trademark/content/image rights;
- Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Shipping, Returns, Refunds, Cancellation, Warranty,
  and acceptable-use text;
- product claims, pricing, promotions, tax, invoices, currency, regions,
  delivery promises, prohibited goods, and age restrictions;
- payment-provider merchant terms, chargebacks, disputes, and refund
  operations;
- privacy purpose, lawful basis/consent where applicable, data inventory,
  retention/deletion, access requests, subprocessors, cross-border transfers,
  breach response, and cookie/analytics decisions;
- accessibility review with real assistive-technology testing;
- security incident, fraud, support escalation, business continuity, backup,
  monitoring, and account-offboarding procedures;
- launch-market consumer, e-commerce, tax, payment, and advertising review.

Record the approver, evidence, scope, and date for every item. An unchecked item
is a launch blocker when relevant. The software and this checklist do not
constitute legal advice or certify compliance in every country.
